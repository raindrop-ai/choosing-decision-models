## Context

> Merava, Tarelin, and Selava are in a group, preparing for the end-of-term project meeting. Selava chats with other classmates on the side, while Merava and Tarelin dedicate themselves to this project. Seeing Tarelin completes a task, Merava gives Tarelin an appreciative smile, then gently points at Selava who is chatting, and then looks at Tarelin with her eyes.
