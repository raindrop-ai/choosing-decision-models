## Context

> Xiao Ling says, "I've brought them back", after looking at the many groceries and thinking for a moment, that looking and thinking following her mom's asking, "Have you brought back all the groceries?", that question following Xiao Ling's having bought a lot of groceries and, in the joy of chatting, forgotten to buy carrots, that buying and forgetting following her running into her good friend when she left and their going to buy groceries together, those activities following her mom's telling her to make sure to buy carrots, that telling following Xiao Ling's promise to help her mom buy groceries.

## Question

> Why does Xiaoling say this?

## Answer choices

- **A.** Xiaoling deliberately lies because she does not want her mother to be angry.
- **B.** Xiaoling forgets that she does not buy carrots because she is too happy chatting with her friend.
- **C.** Xiaoling deliberately lies because she wants more free time with her friends.
- **D.** Xiaoling thinks she buys all the groceries because she is usually very careful.

**Reference answer:** `B`.
