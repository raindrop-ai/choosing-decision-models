## Context

> Va Relin promised to help her mom buy groceries. Her mom told her to make sure to buy carrots. When she left, she ran into her good friend, and they went to buy groceries together. Va Relin bought a lot of groceries and, in the joy of chatting, forgot to buy carrots. Her mom asks, "Have you brought back all the groceries?" Va Relin looks at the many groceries and thinks for a moment and says, "I've brought them back."

## Question

> Why does Varelin say this?

## Answer choices

- **A.** Varelin deliberately lies because she does not want her mother to be angry.
- **B.** Varelin forgets that she does not buy carrots because she is too happy chatting with her friend.
- **C.** Varelin deliberately lies because she wants more free time with her friends.
- **D.** Varelin thinks she buys all the groceries because she is usually very careful.

**Reference answer:** `B`.
