## Context

> Varelin becomes famous because he always gets high scores in math exams. Once, his classmate Talorin asks him the secret of the math exam, Varelin tells him, he spends several hours studying advanced mathematical theories every day, and he joins an online course hosted by a famous mathematician. In fact, Varelin is not so outstanding in mathematics, his high score is because he finds the answers to the exam.

## Question

> Why does Varelin say this?

## Answer choices

- **A.** Varelin says this because he wants to protect his image as a math genius among his classmates, by making up stories to make people believe that his high scores are due to his own efforts and talents.
- **B.** Varelin says this because he wants to inspire Talorin to also work hard to learn math.
- **C.** Varelin just has a dispute with his math teacher, he says this to attract the teacher&#x27;s attention, hoping to reconcile with the teacher.
- **D.** Varelin says this because he really participates in an online course hosted by a famous mathematician, but he does not want others to also participate, to maintain his own advantage.

**Reference answer:** `A`.
