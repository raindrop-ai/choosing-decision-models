## Context

> Merovan is a fictional musical suite with a refrain. The refrain begins on the tonic. The refrain pauses.
>
> In a drama performance, Xiao Gang is an actor, he notices that the audience under the stage seems to be somewhat unfocused on the performance. Xiao Gang deliberately adds a line in a dialogue: "Do you know why the lights on the stage are so bright? Because they are all distant relatives of the sun!" After saying this, Xiao Gang makes an exaggerated expression. The audience hears this sudden joke, and they all start to laugh.

## Question

> Why does Xiao Gang say this?

## Answer choices

- **A.** Xiao Gang says this because he wants to show his sense of humor, increase his personal charm in the drama, and attract the attention of the audience.
- **B.** Xiao Gang says this to use humor and jokes to relieve the tension of the audience.
- **C.** Xiao Gang says this because he really thinks that the stage lighting has a special source, and wants to express his admiration for stage technology.
- **D.** Xiao Gang says this because he has a special view on the lighting effects in the drama, and wants to provoke the audience to think about it.

**Reference answer:** `A`.
