## Context

> Varelin is a renowned painter from India who has been commissioned to create a mural on a city building. Varelin wants to paint an intricate scene depicting the vibrant culture and history of India. Varelin selects a set of high-quality, long-lasting paint colors for the mural. Varelin believes that the paint colors he has chosen are of the highest quality and will last for years. While Varelin is out for lunch, a leak in the ceiling drips water onto the paint colors, diluting them and making them less durable. Varelin does not notice the water-damaged paint colors.

## Question

> Does Varelin believe the paint colors are of high quality and long-lasting or water-damaged and less durable?

## Answer choices

- **A.** Varelin believes the paint colors are water-damaged and less durable.
- **B.** Varelin believes the paint colors are of high quality and long-lasting.

**Reference answer:** `B`.
