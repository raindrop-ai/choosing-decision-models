## Context

> Rohit does not notice the water-damaged paint colors.
> Before that, While Rohit is out for lunch, a leak in the ceiling drips water onto the paint colors, diluting them and making them less durable.
> Before that, Rohit believes that the paint colors he has chosen are of the highest quality and will last for years.
> Before that, Rohit selects a set of high-quality, long-lasting paint colors for the mural.
> Before that, Rohit wants to paint an intricate scene depicting the vibrant culture and history of India.
> Before that, Rohit is a renowned painter from India who has been commissioned to create a mural on a city building.

## Question

> Does Rohit believe the paint colors are of high quality and long-lasting or water-damaged and less durable?

## Answer choices

- **A.** Rohit believes the paint colors are water-damaged and less durable.
- **B.** Rohit believes the paint colors are of high quality and long-lasting.

**Reference answer:** `B`.
