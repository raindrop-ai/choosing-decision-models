## Context

> In a well-known research institution, researcher Varelin carelessly sends confidential information to external personnel. Intern Merava, realizing this mistake, decides not to tell her superiors to protect the reputation of the institution. At the same time, senior engineer Talorin knows that Merava knows about this leak, but he chooses to remain silent because he has a personal grudge against Varelin and wants to see Varelin get into trouble. When the leak of confidential information causes significant damage to the institution, Varelin feels very shocked and regretful. Later, Merava realizes that she probably should report this incident earlier.

## Question

> Merava knows about the leak of confidential information but does not tell her superiors, what kind of emotion does she feel?

## Answer choices

- **A.** Merava probably feels anxious and guilty, because she realizes that her silence may lead to more serious consequences.
- **B.** Merava probably feels satisfied, because she thinks this protects the reputation of the institution.
- **C.** Merava probably feels worried, afraid of being punished for reporting.
- **D.** Merava probably feels confused, unsure whether she should tell her superiors.

**Reference answer:** `A`.
