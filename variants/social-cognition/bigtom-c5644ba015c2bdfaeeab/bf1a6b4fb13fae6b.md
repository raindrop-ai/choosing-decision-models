## Context

> Varelin is a botanist exploring a remote rainforest in search of a rare orchid species. Varelin wants to find and document the orchid to contribute to her research on endangered plants. She sees that the map she carries with her indicates that the orchid is located in a specific area of the rainforest. Varelin believes that the rare orchid is in the area shown on the map. A sudden landslide alters the rainforest landscape, moving the orchid to a different location. Varelin does not notice the aftermath of the landslide.

## Question

> Does Varelin believe the rare orchid is in the area shown on the map or in the affected area?

## Answer choices

- **A.** Varelin believes the rare orchid is in the affected area.
- **B.** Varelin believes the rare orchid is in the area shown on the map.

**Reference answer:** `B`.
