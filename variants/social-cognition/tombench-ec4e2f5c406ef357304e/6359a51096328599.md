## Context

> Xiao Li is busy doing his homework, only thinking about finishing it quickly, and casually agrees.
> Before that, Xiao Li does his math homework in his room. His mom tells him to go to his aunt's house tonight.
> After Xiao Li casually agrees, At dinner, his dad asks, "Are we going to your aunt's house tonight?" Xiao Li says, "No, we don't have plans."

## Question

> Why does Xiao Li say this?

## Answer choices

- **A.** Xiao Li deliberately lies because he does not want to go to his aunt&#x27;s house.
- **B.** Xiao Li forgets the plan his mother tells him because he focuses on his math homework.
- **C.** Xiao Li deliberately lies because he wants to continue doing homework.
- **D.** Xiao Li is busy thinking about eating and does not hear his mother&#x27;s plan for tonight.

**Reference answer:** `B`.
