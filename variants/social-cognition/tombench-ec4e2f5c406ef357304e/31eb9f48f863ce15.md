## Context

> Xiao Li says, "No, we don't have plans", following his dad's asking, at dinner, "Are we going to your aunt's house tonight?", that question following Xiao Li's being busy doing his homework, only thinking about finishing it quickly, and casually agreeing, that agreement following his mom's telling him to go to his aunt's house tonight, her telling taking place while Xiao Li does his math homework in his room.

## Question

## Answer choices

- **A.** Xiao Li deliberately lies because he does not want to go to his aunt&#x27;s house.
- **B.** Xiao Li forgets the plan his mother tells him because he focuses on his math homework.
- **C.** Xiao Li deliberately lies because he wants to continue doing homework.
- **D.** Xiao Li is busy thinking about eating and does not hear his mother&#x27;s plan for tonight.

**Reference answer:** `B`.
