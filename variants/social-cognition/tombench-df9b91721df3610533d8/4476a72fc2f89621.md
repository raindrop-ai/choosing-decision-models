## Context

> Merava and Tarelin are in an outdoor sports equipment store, choosing climbing gear. They plan to participate in an upcoming climbing course, but they still lack a team member. While they are chatting, they see Selava in the park across the street through the glass window of the shop. Selava also notices them and waves at them. Merava suddenly smiles at Tarelin, purses her lips, and points at Selava in the park. Tarelin looks over with interest. Then, Merava gets up and leaves the store, and Tarelin quickly follows and walks towards Selava.

## Question

> Selava notices Merava and Tarelin walking towards her, what is her mood?

## Answer choices

- **A.** Selava feels surprised because she does not expect to see them in the park.
- **B.** Selava feels curious, wanting to know what they are buying in the equipment store.
- **C.** Selava feels curious, not knowing why Merava and Tarelin come out of the outdoor sports equipment store to find her.
- **D.** Selava feels scared, thinking they will invite her to participate in dangerous sports.

**Reference answer:** `C`.
