## Context

> Varelin is a professional chef preparing for a cooking competition. She needs to make a perfect chocolate soufflé to impress the judges. Varelin observes that her oven is preheated to the precise temperature required for baking the soufflé. Varelin believes that the oven is at the perfect temperature for her soufflé. However, a power outage occurs, causing the oven to lose heat rapidly. Varelin notices the power outage and the oven turning off.

## Question

## Answer choices

- **B.** Varelin believes the oven is at the perfect temperature.
