## Context

> Leilani notices the power outage and the oven turning off.
> Before that, However, a power outage occurs, causing the oven to lose heat rapidly.
> Before that, Leilani believes that the oven is at the perfect temperature for her soufflé.
> Before that, Leilani observes that her oven is preheated to the precise temperature required for baking the soufflé.
> Before that, She needs to make a perfect chocolate soufflé to impress the judges.
> Before that, Leilani is a professional chef preparing for a cooking competition.

## Question

> Does Leilani believe the oven is at the perfect temperature or that it has lost heat due to the power outage?

## Answer choices

- **A.** Leilani believes the oven has lost heat due to the power outage.
- **B.** Leilani believes the oven is at the perfect temperature.

**Reference answer:** `A`.
