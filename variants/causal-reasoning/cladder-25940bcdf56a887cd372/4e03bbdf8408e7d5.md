## Context

> Imagine a self-contained, hypothetical world with only the following conditions, and without any unmentioned factors or causal relationships: For those who are rixq, the probability of zuph is 47%. For those who are not rixq, the probability of zuph is 31%.
> For those who are rixq and are zuph, the probability of xevu is 73%. For those who are rixq and are not zuph, the probability of xevu is 7%. For those who are not rixq and are zuph, the probability of xevu is 74%. For those who are not rixq and are not zuph, the probability of xevu is 27%. Zuph has a direct effect on xevu. Rixq has a direct effect on zuph and xevu.

## Question

> If we disregard the mediation effect through zuph, would rixq positively affect xevu?

## Answer choices

- **yes.** Yes
- **no.** No

**Reference answer:** `no`.
