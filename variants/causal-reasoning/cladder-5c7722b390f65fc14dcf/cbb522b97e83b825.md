## Context

> Imagine a self-contained, hypothetical world with only the following conditions, and without any unmentioned factors or causal relationships: Zupj has a direct effect on muvq. For those who are not yupt and are zupj, the probability of muvq is 18%.
> For those who are yupt and are zupj, the probability of muvq is 46%. For those who are yupt, the probability of zupj is 14%. Yupt has a direct effect on zupj and muvq. For those who are not yupt and are not zupj, the probability of muvq is 54%. For those who are yupt and are not zupj, the probability of muvq is 62%. For those who are not yupt, the probability of zupj is 85%.

## Question

> If we disregard the mediation effect through zupj, would yupt positively affect muvq?

## Answer choices

- **yes.** Yes
- **no.** No

**Reference answer:** `yes`.
