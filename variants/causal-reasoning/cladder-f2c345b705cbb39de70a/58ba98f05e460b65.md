## Context

> Imagine a self-contained, hypothetical world with only the following conditions, and without any unmentioned factors or causal relationships: For people who drink coffee, the probability of alarm set by wife is 24%. For people who do not drink coffee, the probability of alarm set by wife is 91%.
> For people who drink coffee and wives that set the alarm, the probability of ringing alarm is 97%. For people who drink coffee and wives that don't set the alarm, the probability of ringing alarm is 50%. For people who do not drink coffee and wives that set the alarm, the probability of ringing alarm is 62%. For people who do not drink coffee and wives that don't set the alarm, the probability of ringing alarm is 13%. Wife has a direct effect on alarm clock. Drinking coffee has a direct effect on wife and alarm clock.

## Question

> If we disregard the mediation effect through wife, would drinking coffee negatively affect alarm clock?

## Answer choices

- **yes.** Yes
- **no.** No

**Reference answer:** `no`.
