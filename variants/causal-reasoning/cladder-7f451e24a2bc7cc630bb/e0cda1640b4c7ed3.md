## Context

> Imagine a self-contained, hypothetical world with only the following conditions, and without any unmentioned factors or causal relationships: There is a direct causal effect from Liking spicy food to parents' social status. There is a direct causal effect from Liking spicy food to child's intelligence. There is a direct causal effect from Other unobserved factors to parents' social status. There is a direct causal effect from Other unobserved factors to child's intelligence. There is a direct causal effect from Parents' social status to child's intelligence. Other unobserved factors is unobserved.
> We know that liking spicy food and confounder active causes high parental social status. liking spicy food or confounder active or high parental social status causes intelligent child. We observed confounder inactive.
