## Context

> Imagine a self-contained, hypothetical world with only the following conditions, and without any unmentioned factors or causal relationships: For captains who execute prisoners, the probability of the prisoner's death is 81%. > The corporal has a direct effect on prisoner. The captain has a direct effect on the private and the corporal.
