## Context

> Imagine a self-contained, hypothetical world with only the following conditions, and without any unmentioned factors or causal relationships: Effort has a direct effect on lactose intolerance. For nonsmokers and are hard-working, the probability of being lactose intolerant is 88%.
> For smokers and are hard-working, the probability of being lactose intolerant is 54%. For smokers, the probability of being hard-working is 61%. Smoking has a direct effect on effort and lactose intolerance. For nonsmokers and are lazy, the probability of being lactose intolerant is 47%. For smokers and are lazy, the probability of being lactose intolerant is 23%. For nonsmokers, the probability of being hard-working is 40%.

## Question

> Does smoking positively affect lactose intolerance through effort?

## Answer choices

- **yes.** Yes
- **no.** No

**Reference answer:** `yes`.
