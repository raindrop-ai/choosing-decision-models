## Context

> Imagine a self-contained, hypothetical world with only the following conditions, and without any unmentioned factors or causal relationships: For those who are xevo, the probability of tijv is 83%. For those who are not xevo, the probability of tijv is 31%. For those who are xevo and are tijv, the probability of gyzp is 29%. For those who are xevo and are not tijv, the probability of gyzp is 70%.
> For those who are not xevo and are tijv, the probability of gyzp is 28%. For those who are not xevo and are not tijv, the probability of gyzp is 70%. Tijw is unobserved. Tijv has a direct effect on gyzp. Xevo has a direct effect on tijv. Tijw has a direct effect on xevo and gyzp.

## Question

> For those who are xevo, would it be less likely to see gyzp if the individual was not xevo?

## Answer choices
