## Context

> Imagine a self-contained, hypothetical world with only the following conditions, and without any unmentioned factors or causal relationships: For those who are xevo, the probability of tijv is 99%. For those who are not xevo, the probability of tijv is 61%.
> For those who are xevo and are tijv, the probability of gyzp is 21%. For those who are xevo and are not tijv, the probability of gyzp is 56%. For those who are not xevo and are tijv, the probability of gyzp is 51%. For those who are not xevo and are not tijv, the probability of gyzp is 75%. Tijv has a direct effect on gyzp. Xevo has a direct effect on tijv and gyzp.

## Question

> If we disregard the mediation effect through tijv, would xevo positively affect gyzp?

## Answer choices

- **yes.** Yes
- **no.** No

**Reference answer:** `no`.
