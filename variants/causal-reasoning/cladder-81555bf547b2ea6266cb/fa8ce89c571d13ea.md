## Context

> Imagine a self-contained, hypothetical world with only the following conditions, and without any unmentioned factors or causal relationships: For those who are yomx, the probability of gwet is 88%. For those who are not yomx, the probability of gwet is 8%.
> For those who are yomx and are gwet, the probability of xevu is 87%. For those who are yomx and are not gwet, the probability of xevu is 59%. For those who are not yomx and are gwet, the probability of xevu is 88%. For those who are not yomx and are not gwet, the probability of xevu is 75%. Gwet has a direct effect on xevu. Yomx has a direct effect on gwet and xevu.
