## Context

> Imagine a self-contained, hypothetical world with only the following conditions, and without any unmentioned factors or causal relationships: For those who are rixq, the probability of swoy is 23%. > For those who are rixq and are not swoy, the probability of xevu is 50%. For those who are not rixq and are swoy, the probability of xevu is 53%. For those who are not rixq and are not swoy, the probability of xevu is 84%. Swoy has a direct effect on xevu. Rixq has a direct effect on xevu. Zuph has a direct effect on swoy and rixq.

## Question
