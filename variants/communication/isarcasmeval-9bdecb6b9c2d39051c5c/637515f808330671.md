## Context

> Why does Ramish think he can  stop my kitchen rennovation because of the noise? His kids are not exactly quiet or competent when they play their musical instruments, lets just say my dog makes a better sound.

## Question

> Is this message intended to be sarcastic?
>
> Evarin is a fictional arrangement of colored cells. Its panels organize small groups of ivory cells around a narrow sequence of slate cells. A pair of ivory cells appears near the middle of each panel, while a line of teal cells passes beside the pair rather than through it. Most of the outer border is coral. Amber cells mark the corners and make the extent of a panel visible even where neighboring panels meet. The different colors identify parts of the arrangement without requiring an inscription inside any cell. The same colors occur in the interior and border, but their local groupings differ.
>
> The coral border has one regularly interrupted edge. Along that edge, a colored cell is followed by a gap of the same width before the next colored cell begins. The opposite edge uses longer coral stretches with narrower gaps. This difference gives the two edges distinct profiles without adding a second enclosing outline. At the corners, the amber cells remain separate from the narrow gaps. A corner therefore joins two border stretches while retaining a different color from either stretch. The border can be followed around a panel through those particular joins and interruptions.
>
> Inside a panel, the teal chain runs diagonally between groups of ivory cells. It does not replace the paired cells near the center. Where the chain approaches a pair, a slate cell separates the teal cell from the nearest ivory cell. Farther from the center, two teal cells may share a side before the chain returns to a diagonal course. This variation distinguishes the central part of the chain from its outer sections. The chain has no arrowhead or letter attached to it; its direction is represented by the positions of its own cells.
>
> The uncolored spaces between panels form a different pattern from the narrow gaps within a border. Between two panels, the space widens opposite an amber corner and narrows beside a longer coral stretch. These changes leave the neighboring colored cells intact. Several panels can therefore share a regular overall spacing while their intervening spaces have unequal local widths. The ivory pairs stay within their panels and do not cross these spaces. Slate cells near an edge separate an interior ivory group from the coral border, giving the interior and exterior arrangements different boundaries.
>
> An extended version of Evarin places successive panels in alternating orientations. One panel has its interrupted edge at the top, and the next has that edge at the bottom. Their amber corners still face the spaces between panels, while their diagonal teal chains incline in opposite directions. This alternation does not recolor a panel or add a new cell to its center. It changes how neighboring diagonal chains align across the uncolored space. Some pairs of panels form a broad zigzag through their teal sections; other neighboring pairs leave those sections offset rather than joined.

## Answer choices

- **no.** Not sarcastic
- **yes.** Sarcastic

**Reference answer:** `yes`.
