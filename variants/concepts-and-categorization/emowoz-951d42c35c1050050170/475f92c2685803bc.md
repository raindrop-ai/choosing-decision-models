## Context

> The fictional suite Merovan is organized around a refrain that returns with a different harmonic accompaniment each time. Its opening melody begins on the tonic, pauses on the second degree, and resolves through a repeated third. The accompaniment alternates between a sustained chord and a sequence of detached notes. Neither accompaniment replaces the melodic refrain; both provide contrasting rhythmic settings for it. The final cadence retains the opening melody's last interval while changing the chord that supports it. This gives the suite a recognizable connection between its introduction and its conclusion without making their harmonies identical.
>
> «Assistant: You are looking for the bridge guest house»
> «Assistant: Booking was unsuccessful.»
> «Assistant: Booking was successful.
> Reference number is : EPWUAWM5.»
> Before the Assistant gives the guest-house reply,
> «User: I'm looking for a place. I think it's called like Bridge House or Bridge Guesthouse or something.»
> After the Assistant gives the guest-house reply and before the Assistant replies to the Thursday booking request,
> «User: Could you book it for 3 people and 4 nights starting from thursday?»
> After the Assistant replies to the Thursday booking request and before the Assistant replies to the Saturday booking request,
> «User: Okay, book it for 4 nights starting Saturday.»
> After the Assistant replies to the Saturday booking request,
> «User: Thank you, that's all I need. Good bye.»
