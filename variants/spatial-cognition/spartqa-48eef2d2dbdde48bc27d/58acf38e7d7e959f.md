## Context

> There are three blocks, A, B and C. Block C is to the left of B and block B is below A. Block B contains a medium yellow square and a medium blue square. The medium yellow square is touching the bottom edge of this block. It is below the medium blue square. Block A contains a medium blue square. The medium blue square is below a medium black square. And block C has two medium yellow squares. Medium yellow square number one is below medium yellow square number two.. One medium blue square is also touching the bottom edge of this block. Below medium yellow square number one and medium yellow square number two is the medium blue square.

## Question

> What is the relation between the medium object that is below medium yellow object number one and the yellow square which is above a yellow square?
