## Context

> There are three blocks. We call them Apple, Bridge and Castle. Block Bridge is to the left of Apple and block Apple is below Castle. Block Apple has a big blue triangle, one big yellow square and a medium blue triangle. The big blue triangle is below the big yellow square which is above, near to and to the left of the medium blue triangle. This block also has a big black square. This object is touching the right edge of this block. To the left of the medium blue triangle there is the big blue triangle. The big yellow square is touching the top edge of this block. Block Bridge contains a small yellow circle, a small yellow square and a medium black circle. The small yellow circle is to the left of the medium black circle. The small yellow square is to the right of the medium black circle. It is touching the bottom edge of this block. The small yellow circle is above the small yellow square. The medium black circle is touching the bottom edge of this block. And block Castle contains one small blue circle.

## Question

> What is the relation between the small blue circle and the big triangle that is to the right of a small yellow square that is touching the bottom edge of a block?

## Answer choices

- **0.** left
- **1.** right
- **2.** above
- **3.** below
- **4.** near to
- **5.** far from
- **6.** touching
- **7.** DK

**Reference answer:** `2`.
