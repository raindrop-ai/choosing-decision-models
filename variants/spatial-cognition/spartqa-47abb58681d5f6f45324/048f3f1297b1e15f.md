## Context

> There are three blocks, A, B and C. There are two medium blue squares in block A. Medium blue square number one is touching the bottom edge of this block. Below a medium yellow square is medium blue square number two. Medium blue square number one is below the medium yellow square and medium blue square number two. Then, there is block B which has one medium blue square. Block C is to the right of block B and below block A. It contains one medium blue square.

## Question

> Which object is above a medium blue square? medium blue square number two or the medium blue square that is in block B?

## Answer choices

- **3.** none of them
- **2.** both of them
- **1.** the medium blue square  that is in block B
- **0.** medium blue square  number two

**Reference answer:** `0`.
