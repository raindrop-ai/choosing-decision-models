## Context

> We have two blocks, Apple and Bridge. There are a medium black square, one big yellow square and a medium blue triangle in block Apple. Below the medium blue triangle and the big yellow square is the medium black square. The big yellow square is to the left of and above the medium blue triangle. Block Bridge is above block Apple. It contains one big black triangle, a big yellow square and one medium blue triangle. To the left of the big yellow square there is the big black triangle. The medium blue triangle is to the left of and near to a big blue square which is below a medium yellow triangle. The medium yellow triangle is touching the right edge of this block. The big black triangle is touching the left edge of this block. This object is below the medium yellow triangle.

## Question

> What is above the medium black square? a big yellow square or a thing that is touching the left edge of a block?

## Answer choices

- **0.** big yellow square 
- **1.** thing that is touching the left edge of a block 
- **2.** both of them
- **3.** none of them

**Reference answer:** `2`.
