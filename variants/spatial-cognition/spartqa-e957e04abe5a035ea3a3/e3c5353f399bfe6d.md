## Context

> There are three blocks. We call them A, B and C. Block A is above B. Block B is to the right of C. Block B contains a medium black square. A medium blue square is below the medium black square. Block A contains one medium yellow square. Block C contains two medium yellow squares. Medium yellow square number one is below medium yellow square number two.. A medium blue square is below medium yellow square number one and medium yellow square number two.

## Question

> Which object is to the left of a medium blue square? the medium yellow square that is in block A or medium yellow square number one?

## Answer choices

- **0.** the medium yellow square  that is in block A
- **1.** medium yellow square  number one
- **2.** both of them
- **3.** none of them

**Reference answer:** `1`.
