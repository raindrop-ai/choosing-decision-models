## Context

> Leah just got a new kitten. Spaying her cost $200, her 3 vaccines costs $20 each, and the kitten broke vases that cost $12 each. Leah also bought a cat bed and a scratching post, but she used a gift card to pay for these items. How much has the kitten cost so far, excluding the items paid for with the gift card?
>
> «Nola pays $150 for an adoption fee and $25 each for two vaccinations. Her puppy breaks an unspecified number of bowls priced at $9 each. A gift voucher pays for its basket. What are her costs excluding the voucher-funded basket?»
>
> «Pavel pays a $90 aquarium setup fee and buys three filters at $14 each. He replaces an unspecified number of damaged ornaments at $6 each. A store credit fully pays for the lid. What does he spend excluding the credited lid?»
>
> «Quinn pays $120 for a rabbit enclosure and $18 for a check-up. The rabbit damages an unspecified number of trays costing $7 each. Reward points pay for the carrier, which is excluded. What are the included costs?»

## Question

> Which missing variable should be asked about to solve this problem?

## Answer choices

- **D.** number of vase
- **F.** cat bed
- **G.** scratching post

**Reference answer:** `D`.
