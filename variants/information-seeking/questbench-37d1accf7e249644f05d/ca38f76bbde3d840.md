## Context

> Tyson decided to make muffaletta sandwiches for the big game. Each sandwich required 1 pound of meat and cheese and would serve 4 people. There would be 20 people in total watching the game. The meat cost $7.00 per pound and the cheese cost $3.00 per pound. Tyson also decided to buy some pickles and olives for the sandwiches, which cost $2.00 per pound. How much money would he spend on the meat and cheese to make enough sandwiches to serve 20 people?
>
> «Della makes wraps for 18 people, with each wrap serving 3. A wrap contains 2 pounds of chicken and vegetables combined. Chicken costs $6 per pound and vegetables $2 per pound. The vegetable amount per wrap is unspecified. Relish costs $1 per pound but is excluded. What is the chicken-and-vegetable cost?»
>
> «Eamon prepares platters for 28 diners, with each platter serving 7. Each platter contains 3 pounds of rice and beans in total. Rice costs $4 per pound and beans $1 per pound; the bean weight in a platter is unspecified. Sauce is outside the requested cost. What do the rice and beans cost?»
>
> «Fara makes snack boxes for 30 people, with each box serving 5. Each box holds 1 pound of nuts and fruit combined. Nuts cost $8 per pound and fruit $5 per pound. The fruit weight per box is unspecified. Separate napkins are excluded. What is the nuts-and-fruit cost?»
