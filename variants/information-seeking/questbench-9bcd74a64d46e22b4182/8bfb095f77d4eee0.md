## Context

> Aiden and 12 of his friends are going to see a film at the cinema, and meet up with 7 more friends there. Each seat has enough room to hold one person, two drinks, and three snacks. They each save a seat and then buy enough drinks and snacks to fill the seats. Additionally, each person decides to buy a souvenir keychain, but they later realize that the keychains are free today, so they don't have to spend any extra money on them. If drinks cost $2 each, how much money, in dollars, has the group spent overall on snacks and drinks?

## Question

> Which missing variable should be asked about to solve this problem?

## Answer choices

- **F.** Cost of one snack
- **L.** Cost of each keychain

**Reference answer:** `F`.
