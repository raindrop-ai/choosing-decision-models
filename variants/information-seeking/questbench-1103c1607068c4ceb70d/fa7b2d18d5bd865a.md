## Context

> Janet buys a brooch for her daughter. She pays for the material to make it and then another $800 for the jeweler to construct it. After that, she pays 10% of that to get it insured. She also buys a necklace for herself, which is not related to the brooch. Excluding the necklace, how much did she pay for the brooch?

## Question

> Which missing variable should be asked about to solve this problem?

## Answer choices

- **A.** the cost of the material for the brooch
- **D.** the cost of the necklace Janet bought for herself, which is unrelated to the brooch.

**Reference answer:** `A`.
