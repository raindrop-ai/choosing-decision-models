## Context

> Jill gets paid $20 per hour to teach and $30 to be a cheerleading coach. She also volunteers at a local animal shelter for 5 hours a week, but she doesn't get paid for this. If she works a year, 35 hours a week as a teacher, 15 hours a week as a coach, and volunteers a week at the animal shelter, what's her annual salary?
>
> «Rina earns $18 per hour editing for 24 hours each week and $26 per hour coaching for 8 hours each week. She spends 3 hours each week in unpaid library work. She works for an unspecified number of weeks. What is her total pay during that period?»
>
> «Omar receives $45 for each morning workshop and $60 for each evening workshop. He runs 5 morning workshops and an unspecified number of evening workshops. Travel reimbursements are paid separately and are excluded. What is his workshop pay?»
>
> «Celia earns $16 per hour for cataloguing and $22 per hour for translating. She spends 12 hours cataloguing and an unspecified number of hours translating. Her unpaid choir practice is outside both jobs. What is her total employment pay?»

## Question

> Which missing variable should be asked about to solve this problem?

## Answer choices

- **E.** Total number of weeks
- **H.** Number of hours worked at the animal shelter

**Reference answer:** `E`.
