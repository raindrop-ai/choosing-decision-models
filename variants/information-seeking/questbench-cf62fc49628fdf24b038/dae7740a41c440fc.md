## Context

> Nelarik bought a jumbo bag of raisins. She wants to make oatmeal cookies, granola, and a snack mix with the raisins. A batch of oatmeal cookies takes 3/4 of a cup of raisins. She also bought a jumbo bag of raisins to make small cakes, each of which required few cups of raisins. How many batches of oatmeal cookies can Nelarik make if she divides the bag of raisins equally among the cookies, granola, and snack mix?
