## Context

> Heather bought a jumbo bag of raisins. She wants to make oatmeal cookies, granola, and a snack mix with the raisins. A batch of oatmeal cookies takes 3/4 of a cup of raisins. She also bought a jumbo bag of raisins to make small cakes, each of which required few cups of raisins. How many batches of oatmeal cookies can Heather make if she divides the bag of raisins equally among the cookies, granola, and snack mix?
>
> «Kira divides a bag of dried cranberries equally among muffins, trail mix and porridge. Each muffin batch needs half a cup. The total cups in that bag are unspecified. A separate bag is reserved for cakes. How many muffin batches can she make?»
>
> «Luca splits a sack of oats equally between two recipes. Each bar batch uses two-thirds of a cup. The sack’s total cup amount is unspecified. A separate packet of flour is untouched. How many batches of bars can he make?»
>
> «Mira assigns one quarter of a jar of almonds to biscuits. Each biscuit batch uses one-third of a cup, but the jar’s total volume in cups is unspecified. A second jar is kept for gifts. How many biscuit batches can she make?»

## Question

> Which missing variable should be asked about to solve this problem?

## Answer choices

- **T.** Total cups of raisins in the jumbo bag
- **F2.** Cups of raisins required for one small cake

**Reference answer:** `T`.
