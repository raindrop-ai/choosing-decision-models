## Context

> Nolan is the editor of a poetry catalog. Its entries are Virel, Zorven, Kesil, Nereth, Pavrel, and Sovrin.
> Nolan classifies Virel as a dialogue. Nolan classifies Zorven as a monologue. Nolan classifies Kesil as an elegy. Nolan classifies Nereth as a satire. Nolan classifies Pavrel as a fable. Nolan classifies Sovrin as a ballad. Nolan reclassifies Virel as a monologue. Nolan reclassifies Zorven as a dialogue. Nolan reclassifies Kesil as a ballad. Nolan reclassifies Nereth as a fable. Nolan reclassifies Pavrel as a satire. Nolan reclassifies Sovrin as an elegy.
>
> In Tate's garden pond, there are 4 male guppies, 7 female guppies, 3 male goldfishes, and 5 female goldfishes. He buys 2 male guppies, 1 female guppy, male goldfishes, and 3 female goldfishes. Additionally, he also buys male betta fish and  female betta fish, but later gives them all away to his friend. Now, how many more female fishes than male fishes does Tate have in his pond?

## Question

> Which missing variable should be asked about to solve this problem?

## Answer choices

- **G.** The number of male goldfish Tate purchased.
- **I.** The number of male betta fish Tate purchased but gave away.
- **J.** The number of female betta fish Tate purchased but gave away.

**Reference answer:** `G`.
