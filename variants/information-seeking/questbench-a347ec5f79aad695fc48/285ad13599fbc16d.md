## Context

> At a wedding Dylan attended, there were guests in the reception. Each guest brought a plate of 15 cookies, and there were also 10 waiters who each brought cookies. However, the waiters' cookies were not included in the bride's decision to give away cookies. The bride decided to give 1/2 of the guests' cookies to the church next door as a thank you for helping in the wedding reception. If each person in the church next door got 15 cookies, and the church also received an additional cookies from a local bakery which were not distributed, how many people were in the church next door?
>
> «At a gala, an unspecified number of guests each bring 12 biscuits. Half of the guests’ biscuits are donated to a club, where each recipient receives 6. Staff biscuits and an unopened bakery box are excluded. How many club recipients receive biscuits?»
>
> «At a fair, each donor brings 8 muffins. A quarter of the donors’ muffins is distributed to a youth group, 2 per member. The donor count is unspecified. Volunteers’ separate muffins are not distributed. How many group members receive muffins?»
>
> «At a festival, an unspecified number of visitors each contribute 10 rolls. Three-fifths of those visitor rolls go to a hall, 5 per recipient. The cooks’ separate rolls remain in storage. How many hall recipients are served?»

## Question

> Which missing variable should be asked about to solve this problem?

## Answer choices

- **A.** Number of guests&#x27; cookies
- **D.** Number of waiters
- **E.** Number of cookies in each waiter plates
- **J.** Number of cookies from local bakery

**Reference answer:** `A`.
