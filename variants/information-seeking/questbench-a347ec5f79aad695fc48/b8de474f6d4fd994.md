## Context

> Each guest brought a plate of 15 cookies, and there were also 10 waiters who each brought cookies.
> The bride decided to give 1/2 of the guests' cookies to the church next door as a thank you for helping in the wedding reception.
> At a wedding Dylan attended, there were guests in the reception.
> However, the waiters' cookies were not included in the bride's decision to give away cookies.
> If each person in the church next door got 15 cookies, and the church also received an additional cookies from a local bakery which were not distributed, how many people were in the church next door?

## Question

> Which missing variable should be asked about to solve this problem?

## Answer choices

- **A.** Number of guests&#x27; cookies
- **D.** Number of waiters
- **E.** Number of cookies in each waiter plates
- **J.** Number of cookies from local bakery

**Reference answer:** `A`.
