## Context

> After all her coupons are redeemed and her class has received apple juice for lunch, how many bottles does the teacher keep? Her class has 29 pupils and she has 9 coupons, with each coupon worth 100 bottles. Another class has a few pupils; its teacher has coupons but does not redeem them and serves water instead. Each pupil in the 29-pupil class receives bottles of apple juice.

## Question

> Which missing variable should be asked about to solve this problem?

## Answer choices

- **F.** Number of bottles of apple juice given to each student in the first class.
- **B.** Number of pupils in the second class.
- **D.** Number of coupons for the teacher with 15 pupils

**Reference answer:** `F`.
