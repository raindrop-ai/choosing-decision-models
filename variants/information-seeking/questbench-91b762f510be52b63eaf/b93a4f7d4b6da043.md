## Context

> One school class has 29 pupils, while another has a few pupils. The teacher of the 29-pupil class has 9 coupons, each redeemable for 100 bottles of apple juice. The other teacher has coupons but chooses not to redeem them and gives her students water. After redeeming all 9 coupons, the first teacher gives each pupil in her class bottles of apple juice for lunch. How many bottles remain for that teacher?

## Question

> Which missing variable should be asked about to solve this problem?

## Answer choices

- **F.** Number of bottles of apple juice given to each student in the first class.
- **B.** Number of pupils in the second class.
- **D.** Number of coupons for the teacher with 15 pupils

**Reference answer:** `F`.
